# My DIY Diary: Creativity, Curiosity, and a Whole Lot of Joy

## Why I Started This Blog

I decided to start this blog more as a creative journal than a formal writing exercise — think less Shakespeare, more 'Hey, look what I made!' Over the years, I’ve hosted countless get-togethers — each with thoughtful planning, handmade touches, and unique activities. I realized that these events weren’t just about having fun; they were an extension of my love for crafting and creating something memorable without spending much money. This blog is where I’ll document those experiences: the ideas behind each event, what worked, what didn’t, and the little joys that came with it.

This isn't going to be a tutorial blog — though I might share some clever hacks I figured out along the way. Let’s be honest, we can always ask ChatGPT for step-by-step instructions these days. But if something catches your eye, feel free to reach out. I’m always up for brainstorming and swapping ideas!

## A Love for Handmade Things

My love for handmade things goes way back to childhood. I would make cards and gifts for every possible occasion — birthdays, Diwali, Friendship Day, you name it. Sure, buying things from a store is easier, but handmade creations carry a personal touch that money can’t buy. Plus, they teach you resourcefulness and patience, which are pretty much life’s cheat codes.

My mom played a big part in shaping this mindset. She encouraged me to do things myself, even when resources were limited. Looking back, I realize how valuable those experiences were. They taught me creativity, problem-solving, and a deep appreciation for handmade art — plus, a lifelong mistrust of glitter (because that stuff never leaves, no matter how many times you vacuum).

## Summers of Creativity

Summer breaks were my playground for creativity (and a bit of chaos too). Each year, my sisters and I picked up a new craft. One summer, we learned block printing, carefully pressing intricate designs onto sarees. It wasn’t just about the final product — we learned coordination, precision, and how to fix mistakes when the colors bled (because they always bled). I still remember how proud we were when we gifted the saree we made to our nani. It wasn’t perfect, but she wore it like it was a designer piece and bragged to everyone that her grandchildren had made it.

Another summer, we tried tie-and-dye, turning plain white handkerchiefs into vibrant, one-of-a-kind pieces. These weren’t skills we learned from YouTube — we figured things out through trial and error, and that made the experience even more rewarding. Honestly, we were like tiny, colorful scientists — experimenting, failing, and celebrating every accidental masterpiece.

## Keeping the Passion Alive

Even now, with a hectic schedule, I try to find time to create. Gifting people something handmade brings me a special kind of happiness. When I moved to the U.S. for my PhD, my mom packed my watercolors, sketchpad, and brushes, reminding me to keep this hobby alive. She was right — creating something with my hands still brings me peace and joy. And on bad days, it’s cheaper than therapy.

## Creativity in Unexpected Places

Even when I couldn’t find time to paint or craft, I poured that creativity into my work. I started designing presentations that were more than just slides — they were colorful, engaging, and fun. People noticed. People loved my presentations, and soon I started making fun, interactive slides for parties and quizzes too. Who knew PowerPoint (or Keynote) could be an art form? (Take that, bullet points!)

## What’s Next?

This blog is my space to share it all — the cards, the crafts, the parties, and the quirky ideas that make ordinary moments special. Whether it’s a handmade gift or a creatively themed event, I hope my journey inspires you to bring a little more color and creativity into your own life. After all, life’s too short for boring events.

Stay tuned for more — I have plenty of stories and ideas to share!
